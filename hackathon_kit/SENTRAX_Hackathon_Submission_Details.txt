2. THE PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Modern police and traffic control rooms face three fatal bottlenecks:
a) Cognitive Overload: Operators cannot manually watch hundreds of video feeds simultaneously.
b) Delayed Suspect Interception: Reconstructing where a suspect vehicle traveled after a crime or hit-and-run currently takes hours or days of manual CCTV review across separate police stations.
c) Chain of Custody Inadmissibility: Exported video evidence is frequently rejected in courts due to lack of tamper-proofing and non-compliance with Section 65B of the Indian Evidence Act.

3. THE SENTRAX SOLUTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SENTRAX automates surveillance operations into a unified tactical platform:
- Multi-Camera Tactical Matrix: Real-time synchronous video grid with vehicle speed detection and traffic density heatmap.
- Real-Time ANPR Engine: Multi-frame consensus plate recognition delivering 96.2% accuracy.
- Suspect Journey Reconstruction: Reconstructs chronological trajectory across multiple city junctions with radar lock and trajectory replay.
- Patrol Route Corridor Planner: Calculates checkpoint distance, transit times, and automatically identifies all cameras covering the transit corridor.
- Active Threat Alerts & APB: Instant sound sirens and alerts for wanted criminal warrants.
- Section 65B Digital Evidence Vault: SHA-256 cryptographic hashing and tamper-evident audit trails.

4. TECHNOLOGY STACK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Frontend: React 18, TypeScript, TailwindCSS, Vite, Lucide Icons, Leaflet GIS, Recharts.
- Backend: Python 3.11, FastAPI, PostGIS, Redis Task Broker, Celery Distributed Workers.
- Computer Vision: YOLOv8 vehicle detection, Optical Flow speed tracking, Multi-frame OCR.
- Infrastructure: Docker Compose, Nginx, GitHub Pages SPA.
